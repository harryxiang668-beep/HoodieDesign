How it works

1

Bring your art

Upload a PNG, JPG or WebP. Transparent PNGs are ideal for logos and cut-out artwork. Add text and shapes too.

2

Place it on the hoodie

Drag, scale, rotate and flip each layer on the front, back or either sleeve. Guides snap to the centre line and print edges.

3

Spin it, check it, export it

Turn the hoodie in 3D to inspect every side, then export a mockup to share or print-ready files at actual size.
